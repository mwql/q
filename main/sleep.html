🧠 How 90-Minute Cycles Work

A standard human sleep cycle lasts approximately 90 minutes, moving through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM. Waking up in the middle of deep sleep causes sleep inertia (grogginess), while waking up at the end of a cycle leaves you refreshed and energized!
